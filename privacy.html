Privacy policy

RevLab: Engine Simulator for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Last updated 16 November 2026 (new publisher Vibalyze OÜ; anonymous statistics, notifications and “Write to us” from version 1.6.1)

RevLab needs no account and never asks who you are. On iPhone and iPad, free users see ads from Google AdMob, requested as non-personalized ads; RevLab does not track you and never shows a tracking prompt. From version 1.6.1 the app sends a few anonymous usage statistics to our own server, which you can switch off in Settings. The Mac version shows no ads.

Who we are

Since November 2026 RevLab is published by Vibalyze OÜ, Järvevana tee 9, 11314 Tallinn, Estonia (registry code 17240903). Vibalyze OÜ is the controller for the statistics and messages described below. Contact: vibalyze@zohomail.eu.

What the app sends to us (version 1.6.1 and later)

Anonymous usage statistics. The app sends short events to our own server, for example “engine started: V8”, “drag run finished, 11.8 s”, “Pro screen shown” or “purchase completed”. Each event holds numbers and fixed labels chosen by the app, plus the app version, the operating system and its version, the app’s language, the device’s time zone offset and the country of the connection. Pro-screen, purchase and ad events also hold the App Store country and the price shown.

  • Events are tied only to a random install ID made on your device (and a random ID per session). It is not your name, not your Apple Account, not a device identifier and not the advertising identifier. A new random ID replaces it after 13 months.
  • The server derives the country from the connection; it never stores or logs your IP address.
  • Raw events are deleted after 120 days. Daily totals that hold no IDs are kept for 25 months.
  • Offline, events wait on the device (at most 1,000) and are sent later. The app works fully without a connection.
  • Turn it off any time in Settings › About (“Anonymous stats”). Off deletes the events still waiting and nothing more is sent. In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland nothing is recorded until the app has shown a short notice in your first session, where you can turn it off right there. If you decline storage on your device in Google’s consent form (see Ads), no statistics are recorded either.

Anonymous error reports. If the app hits a software error, it sends an “error” event with the kind of error, a short fingerprint and the app’s own code locations, so we can fix it. These go through the same anonymous statistics, with the same retention and off switch.

Reminders (optional, iPhone and iPad). In your first session RevLab may ask, using the system’s own dialog, whether it may send notifications. If you allow them, the app schedules an occasional reminder on the device itself (for example “Your V8 is cold”), at most one a day and never at night; nothing about these reminders is sent to us. The app also sends our server the device’s push token with the install ID, time zone, language and app version, for notifications we may send from our server later; that is switched off today and our server keeps no tokens. Turn reminders off any time with the “Reminders” switch in Settings › About or in the system Settings; the app then asks our server to delete the token.

“Write to us”. If you send us a message from the app, we receive the message, its type (bug, idea or other), the app and system details above, the install ID, and your email address only if you choose to give one. If you include your email, we can connect your message with your install’s statistics to help you; we never use it for ads or tracking. Messages are kept for 24 months. This works even when statistics are off, because you send it on purpose.

Our server runs on Cloudflare, with the database in the European Union. Cloudflare processes the data for us as a service provider. We do not sell data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Ads (Google AdMob, iPhone and iPad only)

Free users see two kinds of ads, never on the first launch and never over the running engine: a rewarded video you start yourself (watch it to try a locked engine for 30 minutes), and small native ad cards marked “Ad” in the Garage and menus. There are no full-screen interstitials and no banners.

  • In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland (and in some US states), Google’s consent form appears before any ad is requested, and Settings › About › Privacy choices lets you change your answer.
  • All ads are requested as non-personalized and rated for all ages. RevLab does not use App Tracking Transparency, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.
  • To show ads, Google may process your IP address (for approximate location), an app-scoped device identifier, ad interactions, app performance data and crash logs not linked to you, for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. Google is an independent controller for this; see Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.
  • We receive no per-user ad data, only aggregate reports.
  • RevLab Pro removes all ads; with Pro the app requests no ads at all.

Purchases

RevLab Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app checks with Apple’s StoreKit only whether the purchase is valid. From version 1.6.1 the anonymous statistics record that the Pro screen was shown and whether a purchase was started, completed, cancelled, failed or restored, with the App Store country and the price shown — never your payment details.

Game Center (drag race leaderboards)

Game Center is optional; drag racing works fully without it. If you sign in, your drag race times (with the engine and conditions of the run) and your Game Center nickname are sent to Apple’s Game Center and shown to other players. Apple processes this under its privacy policy. We do not receive or store Game Center data.

What stays on your device

Your settings (language, haptics, units, chosen engine and gauges), your drag race records, ad trial times and counts, the rating-prompt dates, your reminder setting and schedule, the random install ID, your statistics choice and any events waiting to be sent stay on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

The rating prompt

After a good moment the app may ask the system to show Apple’s standard “rate this app” sheet, at most a few times a year. That sheet is run by Apple; a rating goes to the App Store under Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

RevLab is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; ads are requested as non-personalized and rated for all ages. If a child sends us a message with an email address, a parent can ask us to delete it.

Legal basis (GDPR and UK GDPR)

  • Anonymous statistics and error reports: our legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used and fixing its bugs (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time: tap “Turn off” on the notice, or switch off Settings › About › “Anonymous stats”.
  • Purchases and replies to your messages: to give you what you bought and answer what you asked (Art. 6(1)(b), performance of a contract).
  • Ads: where Google’s consent form applies, your consent in that form (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can change in Settings › About › Privacy choices.

Your rights and deleting data

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, the GDPR gives you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Estonia, the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon). The statistics hold no name or contact details; we can connect them to you only through the install ID, for example when you wrote to us with your email. If you give us your install’s details or the email you wrote from, we will act on any request. Email us to have your install’s statistics and messages erased.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something this page does not describe, this page is updated first, with a new date at the top, and the App Store privacy answers are updated with it.
