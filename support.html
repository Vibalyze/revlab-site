Support

RevLab: Engine Simulator for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to vibalyze@zohomail.eu. Please include your device model and the app version (on iPhone and iPad: Settings › General › iPhone/iPad Storage › RevLab; on Mac: RevLab › About RevLab). You can also use “Write to us” in the app’s Settings › About. We usually answer within two working days.

Frequently asked questions

What does RevLab Pro include?
RevLab Pro is a single one-time purchase. It unlocks every Pro engine and the front listening position, and removes all ads on iPhone and iPad — forever. Every “Unlock” button in the app sells this same purchase; you never pay per engine.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I get it back?
Open the Garage, tap any locked engine and choose Restore. Pro works on all your iPhones, iPads and Macs signed in with the same Apple Account, and with Family Sharing.

Can I try a Pro engine first?
On iPhone and iPad you can watch a short video to try a locked engine for 30 minutes.

There is no sound.
Check the device volume and the Volume setting in RevLab’s Settings, and disconnect Bluetooth devices you are not using. Headphones give the best result.

The app feels slow or hot on an older device.
Set Graphics to “Battery saver” in Settings › Display; the sound stays the same. If it still struggles, write to us with your device model.

Drag race leaderboards
Leaderboards use Apple Game Center and are optional. Everything else works without signing in.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
